Your first tandem

A little structure. A lot less pressure.

You do not need perfect grammar to have a good conversation.

Meet somewhere public

Choose a café or a campus meeting point. For a walk, agree a public route and use an indoor alternative in bad weather. You can decline or leave a meeting.

Agree on an equal split

Try 20 minutes in each language. Decide when to switch and ask how your partner would like to be corrected.

20 min your language20 min their language

Have one easy topic ready

  • A favourite food and how to make it.
  • A place you would like to visit.
  • One surprising thing about university life.

Asking someone to repeat is part of practice.

Choose the next meeting before you leave

A shared weekly slot is easier to keep than another round of “when are you free?”. Make the next meeting a suggestion, not an obligation.
