About SprachDuo

Make finding a partner a search, not a coincidence.

A Web-Based Technologies project at Hochschule Furtwangen.

Our idea

SprachDuo brings together students who offer the language another student wants to practise. Levels, shared free time and interests help people choose a partner. Equal time for both languages and a proposed follow-up support a lasting routine.
